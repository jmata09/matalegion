Most menus grow one dish at a time until nobody can say what's actually making money.

  • Food cost keeps creeping up and supplier prices won't help.

How we work

From sales mix to a menu that sells.

  1. Engineer

    We score every item on popularity and contribution margin to see what's working and what isn't.

  2. Rebuild

    Cut, combine and reprice. Core ingredients are reused so the menu is easier to cook and cheaper to stock.

  3. Execute

    Recipes, portions, plating specs and line setup your kitchen can deliver consistently.

  4. Sell

    Layout and descriptions that steer guests to your best dishes, plus server training to match.

Questions

Menu engineering questions

What is menu engineering?

Menu engineering scores each dish on how often it sells and how much it earns, then uses that to decide what to keep, change, reprice or remove, and how to lay out the menu.

How long does a menu redesign take?

It depends on the size of your menu and how much needs to change. After the consultation we'll give you a clear timeline and scope.

Do you work with independent restaurants?

Yes: independent restaurants, restaurant groups, and hotel restaurants, bars and F&B outlets.

Will you train our team?

Yes. A new menu only works if servers can sell it and the kitchen can cook it, so training is part of every redesign.
