How we work

Stabilize. Lead. Hand over.

  1. Stabilize

    Onsite quickly, meet every department and set a few clear priorities.

  2. Lead

    Run daily operations, protect standards and keep owners updated.

  3. Develop

    Coach your existing leaders so the team is stronger when we leave.

  4. Hand over

    An overlap with the permanent hire and a written plan for their first 90 days.

What you get

  • Clear priorities from the first week

  • A structured handover to your permanent leader
  • Support recruiting the permanent hire
