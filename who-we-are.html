Who we are

Hotel operators who became coaches.

The Matalegion Group was built by people who have stood behind the front desk, walked the floors and answered to owners. We coach the way we would have wanted to be coached.

Our legacy

It started with the task force.

Matalegion began by tackling one of hospitality's hardest problems: high turnover and seasonal gaps in leadership. We built a team of experienced task force leaders who stepped into hotels and kept them running well.

Hotel after hotel, we saw the same pattern. When service slipped, the cause was rarely one person. It was unclear priorities, broken processes and leaders stretched too thin. So we built a coaching practice to fix those root causes and help hotel teams lead themselves back to strong service.

Founder

John Mata

John founded Matalegion after more than 15 years in hotel operations and service leadership. He has worked across properties affiliated with Marriott, Hilton, Hyatt and IHG, from select-service hotels to full-service and luxury properties.

He leads our coaching and task force work, and personally stays close to every engagement.

What we believe

Four things guide every engagement.

  1. Operators first

    We've done the job. Our advice has to work on a busy Saturday, not just on paper.

  2. Root causes over quick fixes

    We look past the symptom in the guest comment to the process, habit or decision behind it.

  3. The team owns the plan

    Lasting change comes from the hotel's own leaders. We coach; they lead.

  4. Everyone at the table

    GMs, owners, management companies and brands all need to be heard. We keep them aligned.
