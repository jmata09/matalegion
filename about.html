About

Hospitality operators who became coaches.

We've stood behind the front desk, walked the dining room floor and answered to owners. We coach the way we'd have wanted to be coached: straight, practical and with our sleeves rolled up.

Our story

It started with the task force.

Matalegion began by tackling one of hospitality's hardest problems: high turnover and seasonal gaps in leadership. We built a team of experienced task force leaders who stepped into hotels and restaurants and kept them running well.

Property after property, we saw the same pattern. When service slipped, the cause was rarely one person. It was unclear priorities, broken processes and leaders stretched too thin. So we built a coaching practice to fix those root causes and help hotel and restaurant teams lead themselves back to strong service.

Founder

John Mata

John founded Matalegion after years in hotel operations and service leadership, working across properties affiliated with Marriott, Hilton, Hyatt and IHG, from select-service to full-service and luxury hotels.

He leads our coaching, transformation, menu, training and staffing work and stays close to every engagement.

What we believe

Four rules we don't break.

  1. Operators first

    Our advice has to work on a busy Saturday, not just on paper.

  2. Root causes, not quick fixes

    We look past the guest comment to the process, habit or decision behind it.

  3. Your team owns the plan

    Lasting change comes from your own leaders. We coach; they lead.

  4. Everyone at the table

    GMs, owners, management companies and brands all get heard and stay aligned.
