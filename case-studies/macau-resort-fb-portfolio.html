Macau integrated resort F&B portfolio

A portfolio program across 22 outlets turned four of six loss-making restaurants profitable and lifted F&B margin by 6 points in nine months.

ClientMega integrated resort, 22 F&B outlets
LocationMacau, China
Year2018
ServicePortfolio Programs
Duration9 months

The challenge

Each outlet had its own chef, standards and reporting. Leadership could not compare performance, and six outlets were losing money while sitting next to busy competitors in the same complex.

What made it work

Fixes proven in one outlet were copied to its cluster within weeks instead of being rediscovered 22 times.

The approach

What we did.

  1. Baseline

    built one scorecard for all 22 outlets covering revenue per seat, food and labor cost, and guest scores.

  2. Clusters

    grouped outlets by type (fine dining, casual, quick service, bars) with one coach per cluster.

  3. Shared playbook

    common purchasing, scheduling and service standards, adapted by outlet type.

  4. Reporting

    monthly owner review showing every outlet's trend on one page.

The results

What changed.

MeasureBeforeAfter 9 months
Loss-making outlets62
F&B department margin18%24%
Average outlet guest score3.9 / 54.4 / 5
Purchasing suppliers14065
