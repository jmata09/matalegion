Paris boutique hotel conversion

A Paris boutique hotel completed its soft-brand conversion three weeks early and grew RevPAR 18% in its first year under the brand.

ClientIndependent boutique hotel, ~85 rooms
LocationParis, France
Year2019
ServiceRenovation, PIPs & Transformation
Duration7 months

The challenge

The owners signed with a soft brand to gain distribution, but the PIP required room upgrades, new technology and brand service standards within six months. The hotel could not afford to close.

What made it work

Phasing by revenue impact meant the hotel never gave up its busiest weeks to construction.

The approach

What we did.

  1. PIP planning

    broke the PIP into 40 line items, sequenced by brand deadline and revenue impact.

  2. Phasing

    renovated rooms in blocks of 10 during the low season to keep 85% of inventory open.

  3. Brand coordination

    weekly calls with the brand's opening team to clear approvals quickly.

  4. Standards

    rebuilt service standards to blend the brand's requirements with the hotel's Parisian character.
