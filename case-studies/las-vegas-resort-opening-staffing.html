Las Vegas casino resort opening

A new Las Vegas casino resort filled all 2,400 opening roles in 12 weeks, two weeks ahead of its fixed opening date.

ClientIntegrated casino resort, ~1,800 rooms
LocationLas Vegas, USA
Year2016
ServiceLeadership Training & Staffing
Duration16 weeks

The challenge

Construction delays had compressed the hiring window to 14 weeks. The resort needed 2,400 people across gaming, hotel, F&B, entertainment and security, in a city where every competitor was also hiring.

What made it work

Same-day offers won candidates that competitors were still scheduling second interviews for.

The approach

What we did.

  1. Staffing plan

    built the full headcount model by department, shift and opening phase; prioritized long-lead roles (licensed gaming, culinary leaders).

  2. Hiring events

    ran three large-scale events processing up to 1,500 candidates a day, with pre-screening online.

  3. Speed

    standardized interviews and scorecards so front-line offers were made the same day.

  4. Onboarding

    staggered start dates into training waves so departments were ready in sequence.

The results

What changed.

MeasureTargetResult
Roles filled2,400 in 14 weeks2,400 in 12 weeks
Offer acceptance rate75%86%
Time from interview to offer (front line)5 daysSame day
Opening dateFixedMet, fully staffed
