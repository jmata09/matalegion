An interim GM carried a Cancún all-inclusive through peak season at 92% occupancy after its general manager left without notice.

ClientAll-inclusive resort, ~600 rooms
LocationCancún, Mexico
Year2021
ServiceTask Force Leadership
Duration16 weeks

The challenge

The GM resigned six weeks before the December–March peak. The executive team was capable but new to working together, and the ownership group feared guest scores and staff morale would slide during the search.

What made it work

Three clear priorities gave a new team something concrete to rally around during an uncertain season.

The approach

What we did.

  1. Stabilize

    interim GM onsite within five days; met every department and set three peak-season priorities.

  2. Prepare

    finalized peak staffing, restaurant reservation flow and airport transfer timing.

  3. Lead

    ran daily operations meetings and weekly owner updates through the season.

  4. Hand over

    four-week overlap with the permanent GM, including a written 90-day plan.

The results

What changed.

MeasurePrior peak seasonInterim peak season
Occupancy88%92%
Guest satisfaction8488
Staff turnover during season14%8%
Days to permanent GM start—98
