A 4-star central London hotel climbed from #312 to #74 on TripAdvisor in six months after a structured turnaround program.

Client4-star branded hotel, ~220 rooms
LocationCentral London, UK
Year2019
ServiceService Turnaround Coaching
Duration6 months

The challenge

Guest satisfaction had fallen into the bottom 25% of the brand's UK portfolio. Reviews repeated the same complaints: 15-minute check-in queues, unanswered room requests, and staff who seemed disengaged. The brand had issued a formal improvement notice.

What made it work

The fix was process, not people: once front office and housekeeping shared one live room status, most complaints disappeared.

The approach

What we did.

  1. Diagnose

    reviewed 18 months of guest surveys and 1,400 online reviews; found 70% of complaints traced to front office and housekeeping handoffs.

  2. Plan onsite

    two days with the GM and department heads running root cause workshops; built a 90-day plan with 8 priorities.

  3. Activate

    introduced a daily 10-minute cross-department huddle, a room-readiness tracker, and mobile check-in for loyalty members.

  4. Sustain

    bi-weekly GM coaching calls and a monthly onsite visit to review scores.

The results

What changed.

MeasureBeforeAfter 6 months
Brand guest satisfaction score7185
TripAdvisor London ranking#312#74
Average check-in wait15 min4 min
Brand improvement noticeActiveClosed
