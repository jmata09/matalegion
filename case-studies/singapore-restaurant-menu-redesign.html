← Case studies · Menu Redesign

Cutting a signature restaurant's menu from 68 items to 32 lowered food cost from 38% to 30% and halved ticket times.

ClientSignature restaurant in a 5-star hotel, 140 seats
LocationSingapore
Year2023
ServiceMenu Redesign
Duration10 weeks

The challenge

The menu had grown over six years as each new chef added dishes. The kitchen stocked ingredients for 68 items, waste was high, and guests waited 35 minutes for mains on busy nights.

What made it work

Fewer, better dishes let the kitchen cook faster and let servers actually know the menu.

The approach

What we did.

  1. Menu engineering

    scored every item on popularity and contribution margin; 21 items sold fewer than 5 portions a week.

  2. Rebuild

    cut to 32 items, reused core ingredients across dishes, and repriced the top sellers by 4–8%.

  3. Execution

    rewrote recipes and plating specs, reorganized the line into three stations.

  4. Sell it

    redesigned the menu layout to highlight high-margin dishes; trained servers on two recommendation scripts.

The results

What changed.

MeasureBeforeAfter 3 months
Food cost38%30%
Check averageS$92S$103
Main course ticket time35 min19 min
Weekly food waste140 kg60 kg
