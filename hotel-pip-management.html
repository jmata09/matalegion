Questions

PIP and renovation questions

What is a hotel PIP?

A property improvement plan (PIP) is the list of upgrades a brand requires, often at a franchise renewal, sale or conversion, with deadlines attached.

Can you help if our renovation has already started?

Yes. We can step in mid-project to re-sequence the remaining work, tighten brand coordination and protect guest scores for the rest of the program.

Do you handle conversions and rebrands?

Yes: conversions between brands, independent to soft brand, and full concept changes, including the service standards that go with them.

Do you work outside the U.S.?

Yes. We support PIPs and renovations worldwide.
