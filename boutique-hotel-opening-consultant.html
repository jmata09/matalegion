Services · Openings & independent concepts

Independent luxury and boutique openings, done right.

An independent hotel or restaurant has no brand manual to lean on. Its concept, its service and its team have to carry it from day one. We help owners shape the concept and open it well, so it feels distinct to guests and runs smoothly behind the scenes.

Sound familiar?

A great idea isn't an opening plan.

Most independent openings struggle for the same few reasons.

  • The design is strong, but nobody has defined how the service should feel.
  • There's no clear picture of who the guest is and why they'll choose you.
  • Pre-opening timelines and budgets keep slipping.
  • The opening team is hired late and trained in a rush.
  • The first months after opening are spent firefighting instead of building reputation.

How we work

From concept to confident opening.

  1. Define

    Who the concept is for, what makes it different, and how it should feel to guests and team.

  2. Design the experience

    The guest journey, signature moments and service standards with a distinct personality.

  3. Plan the opening

    Pre-opening timelines, budgets, systems and the team structure you need.

  4. Open and settle

    Hiring, training, soft opening and support through the first 90 days.

What you get

A concept guests remember. An opening that works.

  • A clear concept and positioning statement
  • A mapped guest journey with signature service moments
  • Service standards written in your concept's voice
  • A pre-opening plan with timelines, budget and responsibilities
  • Hiring, training and onboarding for the opening team
  • Soft opening and first-90-days support

Questions

Openings and new concept questions

Do you only work with independent properties?

Our openings work focuses on independent luxury and boutique hotels and restaurants, where concept and service carry the brand. Our turnaround and transformation work covers branded and independent properties alike.

When should we bring you in?

Ideally while the concept and design are still taking shape, so service and operations are designed in rather than bolted on. We can also join closer to opening.

Do you design interiors or architecture?

No. We work alongside your designers and architects on the operational side: concept, guest experience, standards, team and opening plan.

Where do you work?

Worldwide. Openings include onsite time at key stages plus virtual support in between.
