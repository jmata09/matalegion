Leadership training for hotels and restaurants.

Strong service starts with strong leaders. We train GMs, executive committees and senior restaurant leaders, and build onboarding that gets new leaders and teams performing fast.

Sound familiar?

Your leaders are stretched thin.

Most service problems trace back to leadership habits, not individual staff.

  • New leaders are thrown in without a real onboarding plan.
  • Supervisors are promoted for skill, then left to figure out leadership.
  • Departments work in silos and handoffs break down.
  • Turnover keeps resetting every improvement you make.
  • Standards live in a binder, not in daily habits.

How we work

Leaders who build the standard.

  1. Assess

    We look at how your leaders run the operation today: meetings, handoffs, coaching and follow-through.

  2. Train

    Practical sessions for GMs, executive committees and restaurant leaders, built around your property.

  3. Onboard

    Structured onboarding for new leaders and supervisors, so standards hold from day one.

  4. Reinforce

    Follow-up coaching so new habits stick after the training ends.

What you get

Stronger leaders. Steadier teams.

  • Senior leader training tailored to your property
  • Onboarding programs for leaders and supervisors
  • Daily routines that connect departments
  • Coaching tools your leaders use with their own teams
  • Clear standards everyone can follow
  • Follow-up coaching after training

Questions

Leadership training questions

Who is the training for?

GMs, executive committees, department heads, supervisors and senior restaurant leaders.

Is it onsite or virtual?

Both. Core sessions are onsite where possible, with virtual follow-up coaching.

Can you build our onboarding program?

Yes. We design onboarding for new leaders and teams so they get up to speed quickly and hold your standards.

Do you train restaurant leaders too?

Yes: restaurant managers, chefs and F&B leaders as well as hotel leadership teams.
