Legal

Privacy policy.

Effective October 1, 2026

This policy explains what personal information Matalegion Inc. ("The Matalegion Group", "we", "us") collects through thematalegion.com, how we use it, who helps us handle it, and the choices you have. We are based in Las Vegas, Nevada, USA. Questions? Email info.desk@matalegion.com.

The short version

  • We only collect what you choose to send us, plus the basic technical data any website receives.
  • We use it to reply to you, work with you, and (if you're a job seeker) consider you for roles.
  • We don't sell your information, share it for advertising, or use advertising cookies.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time, and unsubscribe from our emails in one click.

What we collect

When you send a form. Your name, email, phone number and anything else you enter, such as your company, role, property location, the service you're interested in, and your message.

When you apply to our talent network. Your current or most recent role, where you're based, the kind of work you're open to, your LinkedIn profile, your experience and, if you upload one, your resume. Please don't include sensitive details we don't need, such as government ID numbers, health information or date of birth.

When you book a call. Booking runs on Microsoft Bookings, which collects your name, email, phone number, the time you choose and any notes you add.

When you join our mailing list. Your email address, and the date you confirmed your subscription.

Automatically. Like every website, our hosting provider receives technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, which is used to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. Fonts on this site are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads. We may use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies and without identifying you.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange and hold consultations.
  • To provide our services if you become a client, and to manage that relationship.
  • To consider job seekers for task force assignments and permanent roles. We'll talk to you before putting you forward to a specific client.
  • To send our mailing list, only if you've asked to join and confirmed by email.
  • To keep the site secure, prevent spam and fix problems.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

If you're in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, our legal bases are: taking steps at your request before entering a contract, and performing that contract; our legitimate interests in running and growing our business and recruiting for clients; your consent (for the mailing list, which you can withdraw any time); and complying with the law.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who process it for us under contract: Microsoft (email, Bookings, and storage of submissions and resumes in OneDrive), Resend (sending email and holding our mailing list), Cloudflare (website hosting and security) and Google (fonts).
  • Prospective employers, if you're a job seeker, and only after we've discussed a specific role with you.
  • Professional advisers and authorities, when required by law, to enforce our terms, or to protect our rights or someone's safety.
  • A buyer or successor, if our business is sold or reorganised, who would be bound by this policy.

Cookies

This site doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies, so there's no cookie banner. Microsoft Bookings, which opens on its own site when you book a call, may set the cookies it needs to work; see Microsoft's privacy statement. We treat Global Privacy Control signals as a request to opt out of any sale or sharing, though we don't sell or share in the first place.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries and consultations: up to 3 years after our last contact, unless you become a client.
  • Client records: for the length of the engagement and up to 7 years after, for legal and tax purposes.
  • Talent network and resumes: up to 2 years after your last update, or sooner if you ask us to remove you.
  • Mailing list: until you unsubscribe. We keep a record of the unsubscribe so we don't email you again.

Security and international transfers

We use reputable providers, encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't send anything you'd be uncomfortable sharing by email. Our providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where the law requires, we rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Unsubscribing from our emails

You can stop our mailing list emails at any time:

  • Click Unsubscribe at the bottom of any email from us; or
  • Go to our unsubscribe page and enter your email, and we'll send you a one-click link; or
  • Email info.desk@matalegion.com with "Unsubscribe" in the subject.

Unsubscribing stops marketing emails. We may still reply to messages you send us or contact you about a booking or engagement.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to tell you what we hold about you, give you a copy, correct it, or delete it. Email info.desk@matalegion.com with "Privacy request" in the subject. We'll confirm who you are before acting, reply within 30 days (45 days where state law allows), and never treat you differently for asking.

California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Texas, Oregon and other US states with privacy laws give residents rights to know, access, correct, delete and port their information, and to opt out of its sale, sharing or use for targeted advertising (we don't do any of these). You can use an authorised agent. If we decline a request, you can appeal by replying to our decision.

Nevada residents can ask us not to sell their information. We don't sell it, but you can still send that request to the same address.

EEA and UK residents also have the right to restrict or object to our processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to their local data protection authority.

Children

This site is for businesses and working professionals. It isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we'll change the date at the top. For significant changes, we'll make them clear on this page and, where appropriate, tell our mailing list.
