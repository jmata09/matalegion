Privacy

Privacy note.

This site is run by Matalegion Inc. (The Matalegion Group), Las Vegas, NV.

What we collect

When you send the contact form, we receive the details you enter: your name, email, phone, company, role and message. When you book a call, Microsoft Bookings collects the details it asks for.

How we use it

Only to reply to you and discuss working together. We never sell your details, and we only add you to our mailing list if you ask to join and confirm it.

Our mailing list (Stay in the Know)

If you sign up, or tick "Keep me in the know" on the contact form, we send you a confirmation email first. You're only added after you click its link. We then send occasional insights on hotel and restaurant operations. Every email has an unsubscribe link, or you can unsubscribe at thematalegion.com/unsubscribe at any time.

Where it goes

Form messages are delivered to our Microsoft 365 mailbox. Mailing list addresses are kept with our email provider, Resend. The site is hosted on Cloudflare.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, email info.desk@matalegion.com.
