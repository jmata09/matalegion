Our Mission

Two problems. One answer.

Shelter dogs run out of time

Capable, trainable dogs sit in shelters every day, overlooked for reasons that have nothing to do with what they could become. Many never get a second look.

Service dogs are out of reach

A trained service dog can cost tens of thousands of dollars, with waitlists measured in years. For many people who need one, that is simply not a door that opens.

We work on both sides of that gap at once. The dog that no one was coming for becomes the partner someone has been waiting years to meet.

How It Works

From shelter kennel to working partner

[PLACEHOLDER: confirm these four stages match your actual program, and adjust the copy and timelines]

  1. 1

    Rescue

    We partner with shelters and rescues to identify dogs with the temperament and health to train, and pull them before time runs out.

  2. 2

    Assess

    Each dog gets a full veterinary workup and a temperament evaluation. Not every dog is suited to service work — those dogs are placed in loving homes instead.

  3. 3

    Train

    Dogs progress through obedience, public access work, and task training tailored to the needs of the person they will serve.

  4. 4

    Match & Place

    We pair each dog with a handler, train the two of them together, and stay involved long after placement day.
