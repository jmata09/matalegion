How it works

From the score to the root cause.

A proven four-step path. Every action gets an owner, a deadline and a way to measure it.

  1. Diagnose

    We dig into your guest scores, reviews and operating data, then meet your leaders to see what's really happening.

  2. Plan onsite

    Two days at your property with the GM, leadership team and, where it helps, owners. We find the root causes together and build a short, prioritized action plan.

  3. Activate

    Every priority gets an owner and a date. We coach your leaders as they change processes, habits and team culture.

  4. Sustain

    Regular onsite and virtual follow-ups keep the plan on track until the new habits and scores hold without us.

Questions

Before you book.

Who is this for?

Hotel and restaurant owners, General Managers, operators, asset managers, management companies and brand field teams. Especially when scores are trending the wrong way, a renovation or concept change is coming, or the menu isn't pulling its weight.

Do you work with my brand's standards?

Yes. We balance brand standards, operating realities and what owners expect. We've worked across properties affiliated with major brands, and our plans are built to meet brand requirements, not fight them.

Do you come onsite?

Yes. Every coaching engagement includes onsite discovery and planning at your property, plus regular onsite and virtual follow-ups. We work with hotels and restaurants worldwide.

Do you work with restaurants outside of hotels?

Yes. We work with independent restaurants and restaurant groups as well as hotel restaurants, bars and F&B outlets. Same approach: find the root causes, build a plan your team owns, and stay until the results hold.

What does a menu redesign involve?

We start with your sales mix, costs and guest feedback to see which items sell and which earn. Then we rework the menu: pricing, portions and food cost, layout and descriptions, and recipes and line setup your kitchen can deliver consistently. We finish by training your team so servers can sell it.

How long does a menu redesign take?

It depends on the size of your menu and how much needs to change. After the consultation we'll give you a clear timeline and scope, including when the new menu can realistically launch.

Can you help with our PIP?

Yes. We help you plan and phase your property improvement plan to protect revenue and guest scores during the work, coordinate with the brand and ownership, and rebuild service standards so the renovated hotel performs the way it should.

Do you train and onboard senior leaders?

Yes. We run training for GMs, executive committees and senior restaurant leaders, and build onboarding programs so new leaders and teams get up to speed quickly and hold your standards from day one.

Can you help us with staffing?

Yes. We help you plan the team you need, find and hire the right people, and onboard them well. If you have an urgent gap, our task force leaders can step in while you hire for the long term.

Do you help open new hotels and restaurants?

Yes. Alongside turnarounds, we help owners design and open independent luxury and boutique hotels and restaurants, from concept, positioning and guest journey to service standards, pre-opening planning and the opening team.

How fast can you start?

It depends on your timeline and ours. Book a call and tell us your deadline, especially if it comes from the brand or ownership. We'll tell you honestly what's possible.

What does it cost?

It depends on the number of properties and the depth of support. We'll give you a clear, fixed scope and price after the first call. No surprises.

What if we're not a fit?

We'll tell you on the call, and point you in a better direction if we can. The consultation is free either way.
