How it works

From the score to the root cause.

  1. Diagnose

    We read the service scores, guest comments and operating data, then see the hotel for ourselves.

  2. Plan

    Onsite with your leaders, we find the root causes and agree a short, prioritized action plan.

  3. Activate

    Each action gets an owner and a date. We coach the team as it puts the plan to work.

  4. Sustain

    Regular follow-ups keep the plan on track until the new habits and scores hold.
