01

Turnarounds

When a hotel or restaurant is falling short of guests, owners or the brand, we find the root causes with your leaders, build a short plan your team owns, and coach it through until the results hold.

What's included

  • Service and guest score turnarounds
  • Restaurant and F&B performance, including menus
  • Coaching for your GM and leadership team
  • Leadership training and onboarding
  • Onsite planning, then follow-ups until it sticks

Task Force & Staffing

What's included

  • Interim GMs and department leaders
  • Permanent placement for leadership and key roles
  • Staffing plans for openings and reopenings
  • Onboarding so new hires perform and stay

03

Renovations, PIPs & Openings

A PIP, renovation, rebrand or new concept is one of the biggest bets an owner makes. We plan it around your guests, team and budget so the business keeps earning and opens or relaunches strong.

What's included

  • PIP planning, phasing and brand coordination
  • Renovations phased to protect revenue and scores
  • Rebrands, conversions and new independent concepts
  • Pre-opening plans and the opening team

04

Portfolio Programs

When several hotels or restaurants need the same lift, we run it as one program, with one playbook and one view of progress for owners.

What's included

  • Ownership groups and asset managers
  • Management companies and brand teams
  • Restaurant groups and multi-outlet hotels
