Built to move the numbers.

Five ways to work with us, all run by hospitality operators and all focused on results you can measure.

01 · Most requested

Service Turnaround Coaching

For hotels and restaurants running below guest or brand expectations. We act as a trusted advisor to the General Manager and leadership team, and we work beside them until the improvement shows up in the scores.

Talk About Coaching →
01 — DIAGNOSE

We review your guest scores, reviews and operating data to see where service is slipping and why. Then we meet your leaders virtually and complete an initial assessment.

02 — PLAN ONSITE

Two days at your property with the GM, leadership team and, where helpful, the management company and owners. We run root cause analysis together and build a prioritized action plan.

03 — ACTIVATE

Each priority gets an owner, a timeline and a measure of success. We coach your leaders as they change processes, habits and culture.

04 — SUSTAIN

Regular virtual and onsite follow-ups track progress, clear roadblocks and adjust the plan, so the improvement lasts after we leave.

02 · Big changes

Renovation & Transformation

A renovation, rebrand, reopening or full concept change is one of the biggest bets an owner makes. We help you plan it around your guests, your team and your budget, keep the business running through the work, and relaunch strong.

Talk About Your Project →

We help with:

  • Renovation and PIP planning, phased to protect revenue
  • Rebrands, conversions and new concepts
  • Reopenings and relaunches, including hiring and training
  • Service standards and processes rebuilt for the new space
  • Keeping owners, brands and your team aligned from start to finish

03 · Restaurants & F&B

Menu Redesign

Your menu is your most important sales tool. We rework it for profit and for execution: what sells, what earns and what your kitchen can deliver every time. For independent restaurants, restaurant groups and hotel F&B outlets.

Talk About Your Menu →

What's included

  • Menu engineering: item-by-item sales and profit review
  • Pricing, portioning and food cost
  • Layout and descriptions that sell
  • Recipes, prep and line setup the kitchen can execute
  • Team training so your servers can sell the new menu

04 · Fast start

Task Force Leadership

Turnover and seasonal swings leave leadership gaps, and that's when service slips. Our task force leaders step into GM and department roles at hotels, from select-service to luxury, and at restaurants and F&B outlets. They keep operations steady, standards high and your team trained while you find the right permanent hire.

Request Task Force Support →

Call us when you have:

  • A sudden GM or department head vacancy
  • A new opening or a seasonal peak
  • A turnaround after an ownership or management change
  • An inexperienced leadership team that needs developing
  • A brand deadline you can't afford to miss
